# Gate C — Founder Test Plan (revised for v0.3.0)

- **Sessions:** 12 over 3 weeks, any mix of stops, times, and sound types.
- **Measure (in-app, once after each natural completion):** "How do you feel now?" Better / Same / Worse. Optional, one tap. The app saves the stop, the time, and the answer, and shows counts per stop.
- **Daily (outside app):** count of irritation moments noticed before reacting.
- **Stop signal (applied by the founder):** Worse on 3 or more of the last 12 sessions → pause and revise before continuing. The app shows counts per stop, not an order, so the founder keeps the running order outside the app.
- **Evidence codes:** effect claims stay X (unverified) until Gate C produces an R result. The frequency and chakra pairings are labels from modern wellness practice; this test does not validate them.
- **Bias note:** the founder is designer, participant and evaluator. The measure is fixed here so results can't be reinterpreted after the fact.

## Why the measure changed
v0.2 asked for two 0–10 ratings (distress and "stuck on it") before and after each session. v0.3.0 replaces them with one optional Better / Same / Worse tap after a natural finish.
- The ratings belonged to the reflection flow (typed or spoken check-in, reading, correction). That flow is gone, and the app now collects no text and no scores.
- Two sliders before and after is a lot to ask of someone about to listen to a tone. One tap fits a sound-only screen and is easier to answer honestly.
- The cost: the new measure is coarser (three answers), is taken only after, and only after a full session. It cannot show change from a starting level, and an early Stop records nothing. Results from v0.2 and v0.3.0 are not comparable. Treat Better / Same / Worse as a rough self-report, not as evidence of an effect.

## Device checks (people, on real phones; not covered by the automated runs)
- [ ] Play: first sound within a few seconds on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome; no pop at start or stop.
- [ ] Listen at the 25% starting volume and at 100% on 852 and 963 Hz in Tone mode: no harshness or spike.
- [ ] Atmosphere on phone speakers and on headphones: is the octave-below layer audible?
- [ ] Turn the dial while playing: the crossfade sounds smooth; the timer keeps running.
- [ ] Pause, Resume, Stop, Escape (keyboard) behave as described; Stop gives a short fade.
- [ ] Lock the screen mid-session: note whether sound stops; wake lock keeps the screen on while unlocked.
- [ ] Android: a tap on each snap and each in-breath. iPhone: no error.
- [ ] Reduced motion on: the glow stays still, no pulses, sound unchanged.
- [ ] Thumb reach and legibility at 320, 360, and 390 px.
